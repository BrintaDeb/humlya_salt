Wholesale Pricing Structure
What kind of wholesale buyers does Hymalya work with?

We partner with premium grocery retailers, wellness groups, hospitalitychains, food manufacturers, and distribution networks seeking consistent,container-level access to authentic Himalayan pink salt. Our wholesale modelis optimized for serious volume — not case-by-case fulfillment.
What are your minimum order requirements?

Our wholesale program begins at one 20-foot container per order, typicallyconsolidating ~9,000 to 10,000+ units depending on variant mix. We supportmultiple container loads and can tailor packing plans for chain-widelaunches, regional rollouts, or distributor network replenishment.
What is the lead time for wholesale orders?

Standard lead time is 6–8 weeks, which includes production, quality control,and ocean freight to your preferred destination. Timelines may vary slightlybased on port congestion or special packing requirements. Advance planning isencouraged for seasonal or campaign-based orders.
How does shipping and logistics work?

Hymalya manages full-container coordination from factory to port of delivery.All products are routed through Hymalya LLC (U.S. entity) for export anddistribution. We oversee packing, documentation, container loading, and workwith trusted freight partners to ensure smooth and traceable delivery.
Does Hymalya offer packaging customization?

We currently do not offer private label or co-branded packaging. Hymalya saltis sold under our own premium brand identity, which is tailored for high-endretail environments and wellness applications. Customization may beconsidered for select strategic partners on a case-by-case basis.
What certifications do your products carry?

All Hymalya salt is produced in a BRCGS-certified food facility and meets thefollowing verified standards:

- FDA Registered
- SGS Certified
- Halal Certified
- Non-GMO
- Gluten-Free
- Vegan
- No Dyes or Additives

We can provide documentation as needed for distributor onboarding or importclearance.
How do I get started with a wholesale partnership?

Submit our wholesale inquiry form or email us directly atwholesale@hymalya.com. Our team will schedule a brief consultation tounderstand your volume needs, shipping destination, and timing — and guideyou through pricing finalization and account setup.
